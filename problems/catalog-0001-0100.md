## JSP-000007: Poincare conjecture

Is every closed simply connected three-manifold homeomorphic to the
three-sphere?

This one-entry reference preserves a relative link in the historical proof
package. It is not an edited copy of the official problem bank. The
[canonical catalog](https://github.com/TheJustinSunPrize/awards/blob/82be4c4913b8fe394d68d1391f4c221fde947211/problems/catalog-0001-0100.md#JSP-000007)
credits the mathematical solution to Perelman, building on Hamilton's
Ricci-flow program, and distinguishes that result from a complete Lean proof.
This repository makes no new mathematical solver claim.
